faq

Frequently asked questions

What is Rivencast?

Rivencast is an AI-agent detection service for bots, AI-generated content, and automated or inauthentic accounts. The agents give each account or message a risk score and explain it in plain language, so people can decide what to do.

Where does it work?

Just about anywhere activity is publicly visible: product and business reviews, websites and comment sections, public forums, and social platforms like Reddit or X, plus company inboxes you choose to connect. Rivencast is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by any platform named on this site.

Do you need API access?

No. Rivencast’s agents read publicly visible activity the way a person would, so detection doesn’t depend on platform APIs. For email, you connect the inboxes you want checked.

Can you see private accounts?

No. Rivencast doesn’t see private accounts, private messages, or anything behind a login without permission. But even when a profile is locked down, an account’s public footprint elsewhere (where and when it posts publicly) can still tell a story.

What signals do the agents look at?

Timing and posting cadence, account age, cross-site footprint, repeated or templated text, coordinated activity between accounts, and AI-writing patterns.

Does it work on company email?

Yes, for inboxes your company connects with permission. The agents can flag bot- or AI-written inbound messages, phishing-style automation, and fake leads.

Does Rivencast ban or remove accounts?

No. Rivencast flags and explains. You and your team review the flags and make the call.

Is a high risk score proof?

No. A score is a signal that something deserves a closer look, with the reasons written out. Real people can look unusual, and some bots look ordinary.

How do I get early access?

Fill out the early access form and tell us where bots are showing up for you, or email inquiry@rivencast.com.

Who is Hana?

Hana is the Rivencast assistant. Access is by request through the Hana access form. Once you’re approved, you can text or call her to ask what Rivencast does or get support, and early-access customers can ask her to check on a scan or explain a flag in plain language. She’s an AI and says so.

How does texting Hana work?

You opt in by requesting access through the Hana access form and checking the consent box. Once you’re approved, Hana can text and call you at the number you provided. Messages are conversational; message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Mobile opt-in data is never shared with third parties for marketing. Details are in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Will Hana text my friends or family?

No. Hana only contacts people who requested access and were approved. She doesn’t text or call anyone’s friends or family on their behalf.

Does Rivencast train its own AI model?

No. The intelligence underneath comes from established language models. Rivencast builds the detection agents, the detection logic, and the explanations on top of them.
