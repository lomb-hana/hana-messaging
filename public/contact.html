Frequently asked questions

What is Rivencast?

Rivencast is an independent AI studio in Houston, Texas, building AI agents that complete real tasks for you, with Hana, a personal AI assistant, as its face — like shopping, scheduling, inbox cleanup, research, and messages.

Which agents can I use right now?

Hana by Rivencast, your personal AI assistant, is available now. Custom detection agents for online communities are coming soon. See the agents page.

Is Hana a real person?

No. Hana is an AI agent, and she’ll tell you so. She has a personality and opinions, but she never pretends to be human.

How does texting Hana work?

You opt in by texting Hana first at +1 (833) 408-5702. Messages are conversational; message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Mobile opt-in data is never shared with third parties for marketing. Details are in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Will Hana text my friends or family for me?

No. The number is only for people texting Hana directly as their own personal AI assistant. Hana doesn’t text anyone’s friends or family on their behalf.

Will an agent spend my money or send messages without asking?

No. Agents check with you before they spend money or send anything on your behalf. Drafting is not the same as sending, and looking at a cart is not the same as placing an order.

Can agents see my passwords or card numbers?

Passwords, card numbers, and login codes are kept in an encrypted vault the agents never see. They’re filled in directly when a site or checkout needs them, without being shown to the agent.

Which accounts can an agent use?

Only the ones you connect and approve — for example mail, calendar, files, read-only bank connections, Discord, X, and text messaging. You can disconnect one anytime.

Does Rivencast train its own AI model?

No. The intelligence underneath comes from established language models. Rivencast builds the character, the behavior, and the way each agent works with you, tuned on top of those models.
