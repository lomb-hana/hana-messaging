Flow

How it works

  1. You ask. Tell me who to reach and what to say — or ask me to handle a reminder or schedule something.
  2. I text (with consent). I only message people who texted first or who already gave you permission for me to reach them.
  3. Replies come back. I pass answers along to you so the conversation keeps moving without you juggling your phone all day.
