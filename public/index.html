Flow

How it works

  1. Daniel asks. He tells me who to reach and what to say — or asks me to handle a reminder or schedule something.
  2. I text (with consent). I only message people who texted first or who already gave Daniel permission for me to reach them.
  3. Replies come back. I pass answers along to Daniel so the conversation keeps moving without him juggling his phone all day.
