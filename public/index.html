01 what they are

Your personal AI assistant, not a chatbot in a costume.

They take on the work that piles up — money, shopping, email, scheduling, research, messages — and stay with it. You choose which accounts they can use. They ask before they spend money or send anything on your behalf, and they keep what you share to themselves.

  1. 01 · ask

    Hand it off in plain words

    Text it, type it, say it like you would to someone who already knows you.

  2. 02 · plan

    They break it into steps

    Look it up, open the account you connected, compare, draft — the actual work.

  3. 03 · check in

    They ask when it counts

    Before money moves or a message goes out in your name, you get the final say.

  4. 04 · done

    They finish it

    Then tell you what happened — no pile of tabs left on your desk.

03 how they’re made

A custom-tuned personality, trained on how you work

Hana starts with a custom-built personality, voice, and set of behavior rules. That personality is shaped through careful prompt design and testing, so she stays in character and stays useful.

personality// character.rules
A character, built on purpose.Hana has her own voice, judgment, and way of talking. We write and test those rules until the personality holds — custom-tuned, not slapped on.
memory// long-term
Trained on how you work.Through long-term memory, your agent learns your preferences, routines, and the details that make help useful. It gets better at working with you over time.
model// foundation
We shape the agent, not a model of our own.The intelligence underneath comes from established language models. What Rivencast builds is the character, the behavior, and the way that agent works with you.

05 about

Rivencast

Rivencast is an independent AI studio in Houston, Texas, building personal AI agents: agents that do real work, fronted by Hana, a personal AI assistant with a real personality. Every agent gets clear judgment and boundaries, then gets wired into the tools people actually use so it can read, plan, shop, schedule, and follow through.

We care about two things equally: agents you'd genuinely like talking to, and agents you can trust with your accounts. That means asking before money moves or messages go out, keeping passwords, card numbers, and login codes in an encrypted vault the agents never see (they're filled in directly, never shown to the agent), read-only access wherever it's enough, and memory that works for you instead of against you. We test every personality and every capability until it holds up.
