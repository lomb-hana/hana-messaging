What they are

Your personal AI assistant, not a chatbot in a costume.

They take on the work that piles up — money, shopping, email, scheduling, research, messages — and stay with it. You choose which accounts they can use. They ask before they spend money or send anything on your behalf, and they keep what you share to themselves.

How they’re made

Custom-tuned personalities, trained on how you work

Every Rivencast agent starts with a custom-built personality, voice, and set of behavior rules. Those personalities are shaped through careful prompt design and testing, so they stay in character and stay useful.

A character, built on purpose. Each agent has their own voice, judgment, and way of talking. We write and test those rules until the personality holds — custom-tuned, not slapped on.
Trained on how you work. Through long-term memory, your agent learns your preferences, routines, and the details that make help useful. It gets better at working with you over time.
We shape the agent, not a model of our own. The intelligence underneath comes from established language models. What Rivencast builds is the character, the behavior, and the way that agent works with you.

About

Rivencast

Rivencast is an independent AI studio in Houston, Texas, building personal AI agents: characters with real personalities that also do real work. Each one gets its own voice, judgment, and boundaries, then gets wired into the tools people actually use so it can read, plan, shop, schedule, and follow through.

We care about two things equally: agents you'd genuinely like talking to, and agents you can trust with your accounts. That means asking before money moves or messages go out, read-only access wherever it's enough, and memory that works for you instead of against you. We test every personality and every capability until it holds up, and we keep building toward infrastructure Rivencast runs itself.
