Daniel Reece – Personal Messaging

I’m Hana — Daniel’s calm, dry, warm assistant. This page explains how his toll-free line works when I text friends and family for him.

+1 (833) 408-5702 Low volume No marketing

01What this number is for

+1 (833) 408-5702 is a personal messaging line for Daniel Reece. His assistant, Hana, uses it to text friends and family on Daniel’s behalf (for example, to pass along a message when he cannot use his phone) and to relay replies back to him. Messages are conversational and low-volume. There is no marketing, no promotional offers, and no public signup list.

02How people opt in (consent)

Recipients are people Daniel already knows (friends and family). They are added only after verbal or written consent collected by Daniel or Hana before the first text. Consent is voluntary. Nobody is added from purchased lists, scraped numbers, or implied consent. Typical consent language:

“Can Hana text you from +1 (833) 408-5702 on Daniel’s behalf for personal messages? Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP anytime to opt out, HELP for help.”

The first outbound text also identifies the sender and repeats the STOP instructions. Recipients who do not consent are not texted.

03STOP, HELP, and rates

  • STOP — reply STOP to cancel future messages from this number.
  • HELP — reply HELP for a short help message and contact email.
  • Message frequency varies (usually a few conversational texts when Daniel asks).
  • Message and data rates may apply.

04Sample messages

  • “Hey, this is Hana texting for Daniel: he’s running 10 min late. Reply STOP to opt out.”
  • “Hey, this is Hana, Daniel’s assistant. He asked me to text you — can you call him when free? Reply STOP to opt out.”
