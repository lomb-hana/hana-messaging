Capabilities

What an agent can do for you

Not a chatbot with a to-do list taped on. Agents take work off your plate — and they ask before they spend money or send messages on your behalf.

01

Handle real tasks

Errands, shopping, scheduling, reminders, research, inbox help, and the everyday things you’d otherwise drop.

02

Talk like a person

Each agent has a personality — opinions, humor, moods — and speaks plainly. No corporate cheerleading.

03

Stay in your loop

They check with you before spending or messaging anyone for you, then bring the results back so things keep moving.

04

Keep it private

Your conversations and tasks stay between you and your agent. No marketing blasts, no selling your number.

Flow

How it works

  1. You ask. Tell your agent what you need — a reminder, a schedule, a lookup, a message, a to-do, a bill to track.
  2. They get it done (with consent). Agents handle the work. They ask before spending money or sending messages on your behalf, and they only text people who opted in.
  3. You stay in the loop. Replies and results come back to you, so things keep moving without you juggling your phone all day.
