Rivencast splits detection into focused AI agents, each watching one kind of signal. They read publicly visible activity the way a person would (no platform API needed), plus any inbox you connect. Their findings roll up into one risk score and one plain-language explanation. In early access.

the pipeline

How the agents split the work

agent.01

Footprint mapper

Traces where and when an account shows up publicly across sites, and the public trail a locked profile can still leave elsewhere.

agent.02

Cadence analyst

Looks at timing: intervals, bursts, and round-the-clock activity no person keeps up.

agent.03

Account profiler

Checks account age and public history to see whether an account’s story adds up.

agent.04

Text matcher

Finds repeated and templated text, including lightly reworded copies across reviews, threads, and inboxes.

agent.05

AI-text detector

Looks for the phrasing, structure, and sameness typical of machine-generated writing.

agent.06

Network watcher

Spots accounts acting together: shared timing, shared links, shared targets.

agent.07

Explainer

Rolls everything into one risk score and a plain-language reason a person can check.
