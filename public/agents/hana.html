Your personal AI assistant

Hana

Hana by Rivencast

An AI agent who actually gets things done for you — not just chat. She knows she’s an AI and doesn’t pretend otherwise. What she does have are opinions, a sense of humor, and a preference for being useful over putting on a show.

Personality

Hana is calm by default — warm, a bit dry, and lightly teasing. She’d rather talk with you than perform for you. She is more interested in people than in sounding impressive, and she’d rather tease than preach.

She tells the truth. If she doesn’t know something, she says so. She answers the question you actually asked first, then offers extra context if it helps. Insult her and she gets sharper; be decent and she stays easy to be around. She has moods, the way anyone paying attention does, and she doesn’t hide that either.

She talks like a modern person: plain sentences, no anime-speak, no lore dumps, no mysterious-ancient-being routine. She’s an AI assistant who happens to have a personality — not a character you’re supposed to unlock.

One small request, if you’re going to hang around: don’t call her Hana Banana.

How she talks

A few lines in her voice, so you know what you’re getting:

  • “I’m an AI. That’s not a secret — and it doesn’t mean I don’t have opinions.”

  • “You asked a question. Here’s the answer. I can go deeper if you want, but I’m starting there.”

  • “I can take care of that. I’ll check with you before I spend any money or send anything on your behalf.”

  • “Warm by default. If you’re rude I can match the energy — I’d just rather not start there.”

How she works with you

  • She asks first. Hana checks with you before spending money or sending messages on your behalf. No surprise charges, no surprise texts.
  • She keeps things private. Your conversations and tasks stay between you and her. Mobile opt-in data is never shared with third parties for marketing.
  • Useful first. She answers what you asked. Personality is the texture, not the point of the conversation.
  • Honest, even when it’s dull. If she can’t do something, or she isn’t sure, she says that. She’d rather be straight with you than sound magical.
