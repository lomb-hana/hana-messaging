plain answers

Answers you can act on

Hana turns what the detection agents found into plain language: what was flagged, why, and what a score means. She doesn’t make decisions for you. You and your team make the call.

If she can’t answer something, she says so and passes it to the team. Rivencast is in early access, so scan check-ins are for early-access customers.

sample replies

What a reply looks like

A few illustrative examples (not real accounts or results):

  • “Rivencast reads public activity, like when and where accounts post and what their text looks like, and flags what looks automated. Want me to add you to the early-access list?”

  • “That account scored 91: forty near-identical comments in an hour, from an account two days old. Your mods make the call.”

  • “Your scan of example.com/reviews is still running. I’ll text you when it’s done.”

  • “Yes, I’m an AI. If I’m not sure about something, I’ll tell you and pass it to the team.”

ground rules

How she works with you

01 · honest
She’s an AI, and she says so.No pretending to be a person. If she isn’t sure, she tells you.
02 · opt-in
Access by request.You opt in through the Hana access form. She only contacts people who requested access and were approved, and she doesn’t text or call anyone’s friends or family on their behalf.
03 · in control
STOP means stop.Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help, anytime.
04 · private
She keeps things private.Mobile opt-in data is never shared with third parties for marketing.
