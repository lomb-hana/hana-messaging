What Rivencast is

Rivencast is an independent AI studio in Houston. We build personal AI agents — characters with their own voices, judgment, and habits — and we connect them to the tools you already use so they can finish the work you hand them.

That means an agent who remembers how you like things done, who can read your email or calendar when you say so, who can check a price or place an order, and who will stop and ask before money moves or a message goes out in your name. Personality is not decoration here. It is how the agent stays recognizable from one conversation to the next, and how they decide what “helpful” looks like for you.

Hana is live: Hana by Rivencast, your personal AI assistant. You can text her directly at +1 (833) 408-5702. Mochi, Dante, and custom agents built around you are coming next. Every agent — live or on the way — is built to complete real tasks, not just keep a chat going.

How agents work

A Rivencast agent is a personality plus a memory, wired into the accounts you choose, with a habit of planning the work and then doing it. You talk to them the way you would talk to an assistant who already knows you. They keep track of the details that make help useful — preferences, routines, the way you like a note written — and they get better at working with you over time.

When you ask for something, they break it into steps and carry those steps out: look the thing up, open the account you connected, draft the message, find the time, compare the prices, place the order if you said yes. They stay with it instead of handing you a list of tabs and walking away.

How we handle your accounts

They ask first. Some things they never see.

They ask before money moves or messages go out. If an agent is about to spend money or send something on your behalf, they check with you first. Drafting is not the same as sending, and looking at a cart is not the same as placing an order.
Read-only where that’s enough. Bank connections are read-only: an agent can see accounts and transactions so they can help you plan, without a standing ability to move money. We use the narrower permission whenever it still gets the job done.
A vault the agents never see. Passwords, card numbers, and login codes go in an encrypted vault. They are filled in directly when a site or checkout needs them — never shown to the agent, and never dropped into the conversation.
You stay in control of the connections. You pick which accounts an agent can use. You can disconnect one anytime, and that agent stops working through it.

How they’re made

Custom-tuned personalities, shaped on top of models

Every Rivencast agent starts with a custom-built personality, voice, and set of behavior rules. Those personalities are shaped through careful prompt design and testing, so they stay in character and stay useful.

A character, built on purpose. Each agent has their own voice, judgment, and way of talking. We write and test those rules until the personality holds — custom-tuned, not slapped on.
Memory that learns how you work. Through long-term memory, your agent learns your preferences, routines, and the details that make help useful. It gets better at working with you over time.
We shape the agent, not a model of our own. Rivencast does not train its own foundation model. The intelligence underneath comes from established language models. What we build is the character, the behavior, and the way that agent works with you — tuned on top of those models.
