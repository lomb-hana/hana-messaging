01 the studio

What Rivencast is

Rivencast is an independent AI studio in Houston, Texas. We build AI agents that find bots, AI-generated content, and automated or inauthentic accounts: the fake reviews, spam leads, karma farms, and coordinated networks that wear down businesses and communities.

Our agents read the web the way a person would. They look at publicly visible activity: when and where accounts post, how old they are, what their text looks like, and who they move in step with, so detection doesn’t depend on any platform’s API. For email, they work only on inboxes a customer chooses to connect. Rivencast works from publicly visible activity and from inboxes you choose to connect. It doesn’t see private accounts, private messages, or anything behind a login without permission.

The platform is in early access. Our mascot, Hana, is the face of Rivencast. You can text her at +1 (833) 408-5702 to ask about Rivencast, request early access, or get help.

02 how the agents work

Focused agents, one explanation

Instead of one opaque model making a call, Rivencast splits the job: separate agents each examine one kind of signal (timing, account age, cross-site footprint, repeated text, coordinated activity, AI-writing patterns), and their findings are combined into a single risk score with a plain-language explanation. Fast, at scale.

  1. 01 · point

    Tell it where to look

    Connect an inbox with your permission, or point the agents at a review page, a forum, a site, or an account.

  2. 02 · read

    Agents read the web

    They read publicly visible activity the way a person would (no platform API needed), fast, at scale.

  3. 03 · explain

    Score + reasons

    Each account or message gets a risk score and a plain-language explanation of why.

  4. 04 · decide

    People make the call

    Rivencast flags. You and your team review and decide what happens next.

03 principles

How we build detection

explainable// show the work
Every score comes with reasons.If an agent can’t explain a flag in plain language, it shouldn’t be making one.
human// final say
People make the call.Rivencast supports businesses, moderators, and teams. It doesn’t ban, report, or remove anyone on its own.
public// data
Publicly visible activity, or your permission.Agents read what any visitor could see on the open web, plus inboxes a customer connects. Nothing private, nothing behind a login without permission.
careful// not a verdict
A score is a signal, not proof.Real people can look unusual, and some bots look ordinary. Scores point a human at what deserves a closer look.

04 how they’re made

Shaped on top of models, tuned for detection

Rivencast does not train its own foundation model. The intelligence underneath comes from established language models. What we build is the agents, the detection logic, and the way findings are explained, tuned on top of those models and tested until they hold up.

05 the hardware

What Rivencast agents run on

Rivencast agents utilize dedicated servers built for AI work: server-grade processors, NVIDIA H200 NVL data-center GPUs, and high-capacity ECC DDR5 memory.

CPU

Server-grade processors

Multi-core data-center CPUs handle everything around the model: reading the web, scheduling scans, and keeping every job moving.

GPU

NVIDIA H200 NVL

Hopper-generation data-center GPUs with large, fast HBM3e memory, linked over NVIDIA NVLink, built to run large models quickly.

RAM

ECC DDR5 memory

Error-correcting server memory keeps long-running work stable and accurate.

MODELS

Shaped on top, not trained from scratch

Rivencast does not train its own foundation model. Agents are shaped and tuned on top of models for detection and explanation.
