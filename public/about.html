about us

An independent AI studio in Houston.

Rivencast builds AI agents for bot detection — agents that read public signals, score accounts, and explain their reasoning so people can make better calls.

01 the studio

What Rivencast is

Rivencast is an independent AI studio in Houston, Texas. We build AI agents that detect bots — the spam accounts, karma farms, and coordinated networks that wear down online communities.

We’re starting with Reddit. Our agents work from publicly available data: how old an account is, when and how often it posts, how its karma was earned, whether its text is templated or repeated, and whether it moves in step with other accounts. Rivencast is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by Reddit. It works from publicly available data.

The platform is in early access. Our mascot, Hana, is the face of Rivencast — you can text her at +1 (833) 408-5702 to ask about the platform, request early access, or get help.

02 how the agents work

Focused agents, one explanation

Instead of one opaque model making a call, Rivencast splits the job: separate agents each examine one kind of signal, then their findings are combined into a single risk score and written up as a plain-language explanation.

  1. 01 · collect

    Public signals in

    Agents read publicly available account data: age, history, timing, karma, and text.

  2. 02 · analyze

    Patterns scored

    Each signal is checked against bot-like patterns, alone and across accounts.

  3. 03 · explain

    Reasons attached

    Every account gets a risk score and a plain-language explanation of why.

  4. 04 · decide

    People make the call

    Rivencast flags. Moderators and teams review and decide what happens next.

03 principles

How we build detection

explainable// show the work
Every score comes with reasons.If an agent can’t explain a flag in plain language, it shouldn’t be making one.
human// final say
People make the call.Rivencast supports moderators and teams. It doesn’t ban, report, or remove anyone on its own.
public// data
Publicly available data.The agents work from what any visitor could see on a public profile or thread.
careful// not a verdict
A score is a signal, not proof.Real people can look unusual, and some bots look ordinary. Scores are built to point a human at what deserves a closer look.

04 how they’re made

Shaped on top of models, tuned for detection

Rivencast does not train its own foundation model. The intelligence underneath comes from established language models. What we build is the agents, the detection logic, and the way findings are explained — tuned on top of those models and tested until they hold up.

05 the hardware

What Rivencast agents run on

Rivencast agents utilize dedicated servers built for AI work: server-grade processors, NVIDIA H200 NVL data-center GPUs, and high-capacity ECC DDR5 memory.

CPU

Server-grade processors

Multi-core data-center CPUs handle everything around the model: collecting public signals, scheduling scans, and keeping every job moving.

GPU

NVIDIA H200 NVL

Hopper-generation data-center GPUs with large, fast HBM3e memory, linked over NVIDIA NVLink, built to run large models quickly.

RAM

ECC DDR5 memory

Error-correcting server memory keeps long-running work stable and accurate.

MODELS

Shaped on top, not trained from scratch

Rivencast does not train its own foundation model. Agents are shaped and tuned on top of models for detection and explanation.
