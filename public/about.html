01 why we exist

Fake activity keeps getting smarter. Detection has to catch up.

The problem we see online, why older tools keep missing it, and what agentic AI makes possible now.

  1. 01 the problem

    The internet has a fake-activity problem.

    A lot of what looks like people online isn’t. Review pages fill up with fake and AI-written praise. Spam and scam rings run whole fleets of accounts. Sockpuppets argue with each other to make an idea look popular, and coordinated campaigns astroturf support that was never there. Sometimes it’s real people: groups that organize to brigade a thread, pile onto a business, or target someone all at once. In the inbox, it looks like phishing written to sound like a colleague and fake leads that eat a sales team’s week.

    • fake & AI-written reviews
    • spam & scam rings
    • sockpuppets
    • astroturfing
    • brigading
    • phishing & fake leads

  2. 02 the gap

    Older detection looks at one site at a time.

    Traditional tools lean on platform APIs, per-platform rules, and blocklists. APIs can be restricted, rate-limited, or priced out of reach. Rules and blocklists are predictable, so bad actors learn to step around them by rotating accounts, wording, and domains. And because each tool watches a single platform, a network spread across reviews, forums, and social sites looks like scattered noise instead of one coordinated effort.

  3. 03 the shift

    Agentic AI can read the web like a person.

    AI agents change what’s possible. An agent can open the same public pages a person would, follow an account’s footprint from one site to the next, line up when and where it posts, notice when accounts move in step, and recognize text that reads machine-written. Then it explains what it found in plain language. It does all of that at machine speed, across many sites at once. That’s a new kind of tool against suspicious activity online.

  4. 04 what we’re building

    Rivencast builds those detection agents.

    Rivencast is a studio building detection agents for businesses, brands, moderators, researchers, and platforms. They work from publicly visible activity and from inboxes customers choose to connect, never private accounts or anything behind a login without permission. Every flag comes with a risk score and the reasons behind it, and people make the final call. The goal is simple: make it much harder for fake activity to pass as real.

    Serious hardware underneath.

    Reading the web at this pace takes real compute. Rivencast agents utilize dedicated AI servers with server-grade processors, NVIDIA H200 NVL GPUs, and ECC DDR5 memory.

02 the studio

What Rivencast is

Rivencast is an independent AI studio in Houston, Texas. We build AI agents that find bots, AI-generated content, and automated or inauthentic accounts: the fake reviews, spam leads, karma farms, and coordinated networks that wear down businesses and communities.

Our agents read the web the way a person would. They look at publicly visible activity: when and where accounts post, how old they are, what their text looks like, and who they move in step with, so detection doesn’t depend on any platform’s API. For email, they work only on inboxes a customer chooses to connect. Rivencast works from publicly visible activity and from inboxes you choose to connect. 03 how the agents work

Focused agents, one explanation

Instead of one opaque model making a call, Rivencast splits the job: separate agents each examine one kind of signal (timing, account age, cross-site footprint, repeated text, coordinated activity, AI-writing patterns), and their findings are combined into a single risk score with a plain-language explanation. Fast, at scale.

  1. 01 · point

    Tell it where to look

    Connect an inbox with your permission, or point the agents at a review page, a forum, a site, or an account.

  2. 02 · read

    Agents read the web

    They read publicly visible activity the way a person would (no platform API needed), fast, at scale.

  3. 03 · explain

    Score + reasons

    Each account or message gets a risk score and a plain-language explanation of why.

  4. 04 · decide

    People make the call

    Rivencast flags. You and your team review and decide what happens next.

04 principles

How we build detection

explainable// show the work
Every score comes with reasons.If an agent can’t explain a flag in plain language, it shouldn’t be making one.
human// final say
People make the call.Rivencast supports businesses, moderators, and teams. It doesn’t ban, report, or remove anyone on its own.
public// data
Publicly visible activity, or your permission.Agents read what any visitor could see on the open web, plus inboxes a customer connects. Nothing private, nothing behind a login without permission.
careful// not a verdict
A score is a signal, not proof.Real people can look unusual, and some bots look ordinary. Scores point a human at what deserves a closer look.

05 how they’re made

Shaped on top of models, tuned for detection

Rivencast does not train its own foundation model. The intelligence underneath comes from established language models. What we build is the agents, the detection logic, and the way findings are explained, tuned on top of those models and tested until they hold up.
