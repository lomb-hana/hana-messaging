Who we are

This Privacy Policy covers the personal SMS program operated by Daniel Reece using the toll-free number +1 (833) 408-5702 and the assistant name “Hana.” Contact: mndan01@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Mobile phone numbers of friends and family who consented to receive texts
  • Consent records (that verbal or written opt-in was given, and when)
  • Message content needed to relay Daniel’s personal messages and replies
  • Basic delivery metadata provided by the messaging provider (for example, delivery status)

How we use information

We use this information only to send and receive the personal messages described on the home page, to honor STOP/HELP requests, and to keep the messaging service working. We do not use SMS for marketing or advertising.

Sharing

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Opt-in / consent data is not sold. We may use a messaging provider (currently Twilio) solely to deliver texts; that provider processes numbers and message content only to send and receive messages for this program.

Retention and opt-out

You can opt out anytime by replying STOP. After opt-out we stop outbound texts to that number for this program (except a single confirmation of the opt-out when required). Reply HELP for help. You may also email mndan01@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
